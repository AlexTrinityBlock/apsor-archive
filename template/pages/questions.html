FOLIO Q-04 Answered · Verified

What morphological features distinguish traumatic resin canals from normal axial ducts in Cryptomeria japonica?

Synthesized Grounded Answer

Traumatic resin ducts in Cryptomeria japonica form continuous tangential bands lined by thick-walled uniseriate epithelial cells, initiated in response to cambial wounding or cold shock. Regular resin ducts are absent in normal Cryptomeria wood anatomy, making tangential duct bands definitive anatomical markers of severe past physical disturbance.

FOLIO Q-05 Answered · Verified

Why does reciprocal rank fusion utilize a smoothing parameter k=60 when combining BM25 and dense vector rankings?

Synthesized Grounded Answer

The reciprocal rank fusion score formula RRF = ∑ 1 / (60 + rank_i) sets k=60 to flatten the penalty between top positions. This prevents an extreme rank-1 outlier in one lane from overpowering multi-lane consensus, ensuring candidates supported across both lexical inverted files and semantic embeddings float reliably to the top.
