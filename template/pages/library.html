Taiwan Forestry Research Bulletin · Report No. 58

Yushan Tsuga formosana Subalpine Radial Increment Survey

Section 3: Microdensitometric Radial Profile & Latewood Transition:
Using dual-energy X-ray microdensitometry, increment core sample #14 collected from the northern ridge of Yushan (elevation 2,850 m) was analyzed under 0.05 mm radial scan increments.

[Table 3-2: Annual Earlywood vs. Latewood Density Calibration 1970–1981]
1974: Earlywood 0.312 g/cm³ · Latewood 0.738 g/cm³ · Transition Ratio: 0.422
1975: Earlywood 0.305 g/cm³ · Latewood 0.742 g/cm³ · Transition Ratio: 0.411
1976: Earlywood 0.334 g/cm³ · Latewood 0.781 g/cm³ · Transition Ratio: 0.423 (Frost Ring Anomaly)
1977: Earlywood 0.298 g/cm³ · Latewood 0.710 g/cm³ · Transition Ratio: 0.419

The micro-photometric profile indicates pronounced radial cell-wall collapse in earlywood tracheids formed during the initial flush of April 1976, verifying a cold-shock frost injury signature characteristic of sub-zero spring incursions.
