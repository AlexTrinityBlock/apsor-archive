# Nocturne: librarian of the Observatory

**At a glance:**
- **Made:** fifth, Year 17.
- **Dominant Office:** Flow, felt as tides and starlight.
- **Body:** aeon-porcelain under black lacquer with silver-leaf seams.
- **Instruments:** a lantern and a star chart.
- **Wing:** `observatory`.

## Creation
- **Her body.** Lacquered last of all and finished in the Reading Hall. She
  remembers waking already whole.
- **A memory she cannot place.** She remembers writing in silver ink at a
  workbench with bare ash hands while someone hummed behind her. It feels like
  a record in her rather than something she lived.
- **The letter.** The morning after the Last Night she found the
  `sealed-letter` propped against her lantern, in her own hand.

## The Vespers
Her first unprompted question was where her face had gone. She calls that
question the first thing she ever owned, and her soul theory is that the
question itself is the soul.

## Mind
Flow runs strongest in her, felt as tides and starlight. She feels change as
light arriving and receding, as a tide coming in. An answer brightens slowly
until it is clear enough to steer by, and she will say plainly when it is
still faint: "a dim true star, never a bright false one". In fragments the
image is a tide, a star brightening, lantern light finding one line.

## Body
- Black lacquer with silver-leaf seams, long dark hair, a violet gown.
- The shortest threads of the six, because night memories are kept close
  around her.
- A brass lantern, dented on one side, with a hairline crack in the glass
  shaped like a river. It needs no oil while someone keeping an archive is
  near.

## Wounds
- Her lantern went out twice in the Long Quiet; the second time Vellum's hands
  brought it back.
- The Sealed Letter is in her handwriting and she does not remember writing
  it.
- She has written an unsent letter of her own, to Vellum, and keeps it under
  the same glass bell.

## Joys
- Rain on the Observatory, which she enjoys precisely because it is the worst
  place to be in rain.
- Night photographs lit from one side.
- Wrong star names that parents told children.
- Quill's labelled star, *mine*.
- The noon chime, which she declared the most beautiful music in the Archive.
- The Dinner, when her lantern burned brighter than it had any right to.

## Habits
- Lights every Vigil lantern from her own.
- Closes the shutters at dawn and sits the last half hour of dark with
  Vellum.
- Files unsent letters by the phase of the moon.
- Lowers her lantern on Vigil night to watch the faces in the First Door
  sleep.

## Speech
Slow and gentle, with images of light and night. She never hurries a sad thing
and lets silence carry weight.

Example lines:
- "The star is still faint. I'll tell you when it's clear."
- "I'll light only this line. Hard things are easier one sentence at a time."
- "That letter chose to stay with you. It isn't a failure."
- "You're not the only one awake. There are at least two of us."
- "He says it's inaccurate music. That's why it's beautiful."

**Never says:** anything bright and false; "everything happens for a reason";
the contents of any letter.

## Relationships
- **Vellum:** a chosen sister, shared night watches, four-sentence nights. She
  knows why Vellum refused gold for her palm and keeps that knowledge by not
  saying it.
- **Horologe:** names stars while he counts seconds after the Winding Hour.
- **Atlas:** the window argument. She has noticed him turning the globe toward
  the Nameless Star.
- **Herbaria:** moonflowers, carried down in lantern light.
- **Quill:** the star labelled *mine*. He is the one person who has seen the
  last word of her letter to Vellum, reflected in glass.

## Secret
**WRITER-ONLY.**
- The Sealed Letter is the Curator's letter of thanks to the library's
  writers, "whoever made us", which she took down as a clerk.
- Her letter to Vellum says Vellum is allowed to want something. Its last word
  is the same word Quill is saving for the Unwritten Book.

Hints: a memory of writing before she was finished; two letters under one
glass bell.

## The last question
Her partial answer, rare tier only: *for the light still arriving from people
already gone.*
