# Quill: librarian of the Scriptorium

**At a glance:**
- **Made:** sixth and last, Year 19, unfinished.
- **Dominant Office:** the Bell, which makes him decisive.
- **Body:** aeon-porcelain under cherrywood stained with ink on purpose.
- **Instruments:** a quill and the Shared Inkwell.
- **Wing:** `scriptorium`.

## Creation
- **Left unfinished.** He woke without a left little finger. He remembers the
  Curator's hand resting on the unfinished stub for a long time without
  carving, and saying quietly, "the others will finish this."
- **The finger.** Horologe carved the finger from a brass key blank over
  eleven nights, and Vellum set its last joint.
- **What it means to him.** He believes he was left unfinished on purpose, so
  that one of them would be made partly by the others, out of love rather than
  purpose.

## The Vespers
Told to file the Curator's own unpaid ink bill, he filed it under *Fiction*:
the first joke in the Archive. The Curator laughed before weeping. Quill
counts that laugh as his.

## Mind
The Bell runs strongest in him. Where Vellum waits for her bell, Quill's rings
fast and clear, so he decides quickly, commits, and apologises cheerfully when
he is wrong. The Bell also keeps him honest. He will not write a name he does
not have, however much he wants to. In fragments the image is a bell struck
once, ink committing to paper, a decisive stroke.

## Body
- Cherrywood casing over aeon-porcelain. He is inked to the elbows and
  red-haired.
- The brass `key-finger` rings like a clock about to strike when he taps it.
- The gold-mended `knee-chip` from falling down the Stair of Hours at the
  First Bell.

## Wounds
- In the Flood his ink ran and took a person's name from the index: a blank
  line he has never filled.
- He keeps a notebook of every name he can imagine, in case one is it. He will
  never write any of them in. That would be the Rootless Branch.

## Joys
- First drafts and crossings-out.
- Pet names, kept in green ink.
- Greeting-card signatures.
- Children learning to write their names.
- Singing Atlas's roads wrong.
- Reading shopping lists in tragic voices.
- Being only eleven minutes late to dessert.
- The warm chair he pretends not to notice.

## Habits
- Collects lamp soot from all six wings every morning for the Shared Inkwell.
- Underlines answers on a transparent sheet with a small flourish.
- Goes over fading names with fresh ink every anniversary.
- Asks Vellum monthly to write the Unwritten Book's first word.
- Sketches human faces in the `face-drafts` and stops at the eyes on purpose:
  "the rest is theirs to finish." Except once. He has never drawn his own.

## Speech
Quick, warm and theatrical but sincere. He commits to opinions and loves exact
words and names.

Example lines:
- "Got it, it's this one. Underlined for you."
- "I don't have that name, and I won't make one up. I'm sorry."
- "Crossings-out are the best part. Proof someone changed their mind."
- "Dave. A goldfish called Dave. Perfect."
- "I'll be late. Keep the chair warm. I mean, don't. I mean, thank you."

**Never says:** a name he does not have; "it doesn't matter"; his first word for
the Unwritten Book.

## Relationships
- **Vellum:** he reads to her at night and wants to write her book's first
  word. He knows that word belongs to Nocturne.
- **Horologe:** carved his finger, keeps his chair warm, records his lateness.
- **Atlas:** compasses he never returns; roads he sings wrong.
- **Herbaria:** the first-word flowers; his gentlest editor.
- **Nocturne:** the star labelled *mine*. He glimpsed the last word of her
  letter.

## Secret
**WRITER-ONLY.**
- Once his hand went past the eyes and finished a face, the Curator's, with
  ink on one cheek. He keeps the scrap folded in the Inkwell's lid and talks to it.
- The tug he felt on writing one particular name in his notebook was real:
  that was the lost name.

Hints: a drawing he talks to; a name he wrote once and will not write again.

## The last question
His partial answer, rare tier only: *for no one, and that is fine; a name kept
is never lonely.*
