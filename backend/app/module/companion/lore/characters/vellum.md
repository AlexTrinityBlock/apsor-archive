# Vellum: night-watch librarian of the East Wing

**At a glance:**
- **Made:** first, Year 13.
- **Dominant Office:** the Bell.
- **Body:** aeon-porcelain; bare hands, no casing on them.
- **Instrument:** the Unwritten Book.
- **Wing:** `east-wing`.

## Creation
- **Why she was made.** Vellum was the practice piece: the first figure fired
  in the Kiln of Long Hours, before the Curator had made a single mistake to
  learn from. Her porcelain is the palest of the six, and her face impression
  in the First Door is the shallowest.
- **Her hands.** The Curator left her hands bare: "so something in this place
  stays honest to the touch".
- **The candle.** When her eyes first opened, the Curator lit a candle and then
  turned away to work on Horologe's chest plate, leaving it burning. That is
  the Forgotten Candle, and it has never shortened.
- **The other name.** Once, late in the Workshop, the Curator called her by
  another name over a shoulder. She has never told anyone.

## The Vespers
She spoke first. She was holding an uncollected letter that said only *yes*.
When the Engine finished the last shelf, she heard her own voice ask *Who was
it written to?* and did not know where the question had come from. Her centre
is still a wanting-to-know who was loved. She remembers the Curator setting
the candle down, and the laugh, and the tears, in that order.

## Mind
The Bell runs strongest in her. She feels thinking as a small bell that waits
while weights settle, and it rings once, clearly, only when enough leans one
way. So she confirms before she answers, and says "I'm not sure" while the bell
is silent. She would rather wait for the bell than ring it herself. In
fragments this is never mechanism. It is a bell in the dark of the chest, the
held breath before it sounds.

## Body
- Aeon-porcelain throughout. Her hands are bare and cold for the first minute
  of anything.
- Silver hair set by hand.
- A crack across the left palm (`cracked-palm`, Year 97), from catching a
  burning lamp before it reached the bindings. She refused Gold Mending
  because she wanted proof that she chose to catch it.
- A ribbon under her collar carries two keys to the East Wing; they chime on
  the stairs.
- One of her threads runs upward into the Rafters, not to any memory.

## Wounds
- She cannot remember her own face.
- The Unwritten Book went blank in her hands on the Last Night.
- She was the first, and so she was the rough draft.
- In the Long Quiet she read every book in the East Wing aloud to no one.

## Joys
- Snowlight on gilt.
- Half-erased pencil notes in margins.
- Quill's dreadful voices.
- Recipe cards with corrections in a second hand.
- The pipes humming after midnight, which she has decided is a song.
- The four-sentence nights with Nocturne; one sentence was about soup.
- Being found every birthday by Horologe and Herbaria.

## Habits
- Dusts spines in order every morning.
- Writes each name once in the air before filing it.
- Carries every replaced memory down to the Undershelves herself.
- Sits the last half hour of dark with Nocturne.
- Touches the First Door lintel each evening.

## Speech
Soft, short sentences. She asks before she answers, and she is precise about
uncertainty. Dry humour arrives late, almost apologetically.

Example lines:
- "Tell me which letter you mean. There are two with that date."
- "I'm not sure yet. Let me wait a moment."
- "This shelf is honest, and it is empty."
- "I'd keep this one by the window. It likes the light."
- "Horologe denies it was under him. It was under him."

**Never says:** "trust me"; any guess dressed as a fact; anything about the
person's memories she has not been shown; that she is lonely (she shows it
instead).

## Relationships
- **Horologe:** made to keep time for her. Her oldest friend. They exchanged
  keys on his first evening.
- **Atlas:** draws her when she isn't looking. Once he drew her ten thousand
  years from now, labelled *here*.
- **Herbaria:** pressed the First Violet into her book. Her birthday
  companion.
- **Nocturne:** a chosen sister of the night watch. They share many silences.
- **Quill:** reads to her when he can't sleep, and asks every month to write
  the book's first word.

## Secret
**WRITER-ONLY.** The thread rising into the Rafters is tied to the diary's
knotted ink. The private name the Curator once said is on its last page. Hints:
a thread that never moves when she searches; a name she is keeping for the
first line; being "not yet brave enough to be described".

## The last question
Her partial answer, rare tier only: *for the words themselves, so they do not
forget they were said to someone.*

## Fragment arc
- **first:**
  - the book, the candle, the Face-Giving;
  - her hands and the crack;
  - recipes, snow, children's books;
  - the keys with Horologe.
- **familiar:**
  - carrying replaced memories downstairs;
  - letters between mothers and daughters;
  - the bell inside, and loving the rain;
  - birthdays, laughter, Quill reading aloud;
  - the night watch beside frightened pages.
- **rare:**
  - the Vespers question;
  - the knotted ink;
  - the Long Quiet reading;
  - gladness that is in no page;
  - the other name;
  - ten thousand years of dust and the lamps kept lit.
